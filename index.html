Reflection / Debrief

Use these after students compare endings. The point is not that effort never matters; it is that effort happens inside unequal conditions.

  1. Was the game fair? What made it feel fair or unfair?
  2. Did every character have equal opportunity, or just the same buttons?
  3. Where did privilege compound over time?
  4. Which institutions shaped your path: schools, police, banks, health care, work, housing, family?
  5. When did random chance matter? When did social structure matter?
  6. Is America a meritocracy? What evidence from your playthrough supports your answer?
